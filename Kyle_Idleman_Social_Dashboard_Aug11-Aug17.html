Reporting Period Aug 11 – Aug 17, 2026 7 days, vs. Aug 4 – Aug 10
Biggest Win

The strongest week on this account by a wide margin. Net audience growth hit 2,257, four times last week's 564, with Facebook adding 1,956 alone. Impressions rose 59.6% to 341,782 and video views 32.5% to 154,484, all on 9 posts instead of 17.

Biggest Opportunity

Engagement rate fell to 5.1% because reach grew far faster than engagement, which is what happens when one post travels widely. Post link clicks stayed at 8, so a week that reached 341,782 impressions and gained 2,257 followers sent almost no one anywhere.

Recommended Action

Find out what drove the Facebook spike and repeat the format. Two posts produced 248,479 impressions and 1,956 followers. With that much new audience arriving, this is the week to put a GMHC or Food Pack link in front of them.

Note on the export settings. This export was also set to compare against Aug 11 – 18, 2025 rather than the prior week, so every percentage inside the PDF is year over year. The KPI cards below were recalculated week over week against Aug 4 – Aug 10 for continuity. Treat the year-over-year figures with extra caution on this account: Instagram shows no 2025 comparison at all (Sprout returns a dash for Instagram views and engagement rate), which suggests the Instagram profile was not being tracked last August. That makes headline year-over-year numbers like audience up 68.5% and net growth up 1,735% partly an artifact of adding a second profile rather than real growth. The two-format pattern holds for a third week. "God's timing frustrates me sometimes" is the strongest distribution post recorded on this account: 142 shares and a 0.217% save rate, higher than the previous best of 0.172%. It reached 73,350 views, three times the Philippians graphic, on the same crossposted setup. Meanwhile the worship Reel reached the least of the three but produced 13 follows against the graphic's 5, converting viewers to followers about eleven times better per view. Vulnerable first-person statements travel. Video converts. Running both is what produced a 2,257 follower week.
Data caveat. Instagram rounds large figures (2K likes), so derived rates for the top post carry a few percent of error. The two graphics are crossposted so their Views include Facebook while Viewers appears to be Instagram only, which is why "God's timing" shows 5.76 views per viewer against the Reel's 1.54; do not read that as repeat viewing. Only 3 of the week's 7 Instagram posts were captured, so this is a sample rather than a ranking.

Strategic Insights

Wins

  • Net audience growth reached 2,257, four times last week, with Facebook adding 1,956 from just 2 posts.
  • Impressions rose 59.6% and video views 32.5% on roughly half the posting volume, lifting impressions per post 201.5%.
  • "God's timing frustrates me sometimes" set a new high for shares (142) and save rate (0.217%) on this account.

Areas for Improvement

  • Engagement rate eased to 5.1% as reach outpaced engagement. Worth watching whether it recovers once this week's spike settles.
  • Link clicks held at 8 in a week that reached 341,782 impressions, so there is a clear opening for a call to action.
  • Instagram Reel views eased slightly to 56,135, and Instagram added 301 followers against Facebook's 1,956.

Recommended Content Strategy

  • Identify the Facebook post behind the spike and rebuild the format. Two posts drove 248,479 impressions and 1,956 followers.
  • Keep the vulnerable first-person statement graphic as a weekly fixture, paired with a Reel for follower conversion.
  • Put a GMHC or Food Pack link in front of this audience now, while 2,257 new followers are arriving and the Sept 20 launch is five weeks out.
